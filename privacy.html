A short note on privacy.

This is a personal site. I don’t collect, store, or sell information about the people who visit.

What this site does

ryandfinch.com is a static website. It serves a handful of HTML pages, a stylesheet, a script, and a few images. There is no database behind it, no contact form, no newsletter signup, and no analytics or tracking pixels.

The site doesn’t set its own cookies. The only third-party request it makes is to Google Fonts, which loads the typefaces used here. When your browser fetches those fonts, Google may log standard request information such as your IP address and user-agent string. That data is governed by Google’s own privacy policy, not mine.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel records basic request information (IP address, timestamp, page requested, user-agent) for security and performance reasons. That data sits with Vercel and is covered by Vercel’s privacy policy.

External links

A few pages link out to LinkedIn and Amazon. Once you click through, you’re on their site and their privacy practices apply. I don’t receive any data back from those visits.

Contact

The best way to reach me is through LinkedIn, which is linked in the navigation. If you write to me there, the conversation lives on LinkedIn and is governed by their terms.

Changes

If anything about how this site works changes, for example, if I add a contact form or any analytics later, I’ll update this page to reflect that.
